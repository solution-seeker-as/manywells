# Example PVT tables

Tables in the format of `specs/model/pvt/table.md`, which `PVTTable.read_csv` reads: `# key: value` lines, then one row per node of a rectangular $(p, T)$ grid, in bar, K and SI units. `rho_o` and `rho_g` are the stock-tank oil density and the gas density at standard conditions that $R_{so}$, $B_o$ and $Z$ refer to; $Z$ is the gas law's with the gas constant of `rho_g` (PVT-TAB-1). None is from a real fluid, and none comes from third-party data.

| File | Fluid | Grid | Columns | Written by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `vazquez_beggs.csv` | develop's black oil: API 35, gas gravity 0.75, GOR 120 Sm³/Sm³, capped at its bubble point, 235.688 bar, where $R_{so}$ is the GOR at 360 K; $Z$ from DAK | 1, 10, ..., 400 bar and $p_b$ (42); 280 to 400 K by 10 (13) | `R_so`, `B_o`, `Z` | `specs/tools/make_pvt_tables.py` |
| `synthetic_eos_oil.csv` | a synthetic oil in NeqSim 3.4.0's Peng–Robinson with the Peneloux correction, separated in one stage at standard conditions: stock-tank oil 866.4 kg/m³, gas 1.0354 kg/Sm³ (gravity 0.845), GOR 108.73 Sm³/Sm³; bubble point 109.3 bar at 280 K to 200.8 bar at 400 K | 1, 10, ..., 400 bar and each temperature's $p_b$ (54); 280 to 400 K by 10 (13) | `R_so`, `B_o`, `Z`, `mu_o`, `mu_g`, `sigma` | `plans/evidence/pvt_table_eos.py` (needs NeqSim and Java) |
| `synthetic_eos_oil_360K.csv` | the same fluid at 360 K only, without the viscosities and the surface tension | 10, 30, ..., 170, 178.346 ($p_b$), 200, 250, ..., 400 bar (15) | `R_so`, `B_o`, `Z` | `specs/tools/make_pvt_tables.py`, from `synthetic_eos_oil.csv` |
| `synthetic_eos_oil_gor.csv` | the same stream recombined from its own separator gas and stock-tank oil at 0.6, 0.8, 1, 1.25 and 1.5 times its GOR: 65.24 to 163.09 Sm³/Sm³, the same stock-tank densities | per GOR, its own grid: 1, 10, ..., 400 bar and its bubble points (48); 280 to 400 K by 20 (7) | `gor` and all six | `plans/evidence/pvt_table_eos.py gor_table` (needs NeqSim and Java) |

What each exercises:

- `vazquez_beggs.csv`: a table whose source ManyWells has, so that a test can compare the table with it, and check that a fresh tabulation reproduces the file.
- `synthetic_eos_oil.csv`: the shape of an equation of state's $R_{so}(p)$, which a one-gravity black oil cannot follow; negative $R_{so}$ and $B_o < 1$ at low pressure and high temperature, where the free gas carries components that are oil at standard conditions (PVT-TAB-6); a table's own viscosities and surface tension.
- `synthetic_eos_oil_360K.csv`: a one-temperature table at a laboratory report's spacing, read independently of $T$, with the correlations where columns are missing.
- `synthetic_eos_oil_gor.csv`: a GOR axis (PVT-TAB-7), each GOR on its own grid. Its $R_{so}$ is negative at low pressure and high temperature, so it carries its own viscosities and surface tension (PVT-TAB-1).

`specs/tools/make_pvt_tables.py` writes its two tables with `PVTTable.write_csv`, at full precision, so that a read gives the values back exactly. The two NeqSim tables are regenerated by hand (`plans/evidence/pvt_table_eos.py`'s docstring); a rerun reproduces them byte for byte with NeqSim 3.4.0.
